## Follow-up: HUP and cancellation correctness

- The follow-up transport commit keeps an active descriptor registered under a
  synchronized ownership gate. Cancellation holds that gate while issuing
  `shutdown(SHUT_RDWR)`; only the worker closes the descriptor under the same
  gate, so a reused descriptor number cannot receive a late shutdown.
- Read waits treat `POLLHUP` as readable and drain/observe EOF, while
  connect/write waits still classify HUP as daemon-unavailable. EINTR handling
  remains iterative. Cancellation is checked before and after the detached
  blocking worker, so it wins completion races.
- The direct harness passes 28 core XCTest cases and one app XCTest case. New
  coverage proves HUP-only incomplete EOF is malformed, cancellation shuts down
  and unblocks a waiting fake AF_UNIX server, and the ownership seam serializes
  shutdown before close and rejects cancel-before-register without a real sleep.
